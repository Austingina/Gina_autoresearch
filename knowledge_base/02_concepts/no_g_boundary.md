# No-G Boundary

The no-G boundary is the regime where a policy can solve or generalize manipulation without explicit G input.

Current boundary tests:

- Phase 0: mask+depth on random cube in IsaacLab S1/B1 settings.
- Phase 1: CALVIN dual-camera RGB policy using `rgb_static` and `rgb_gripper`.

A strong no-G baseline is required before claiming that G helps.
