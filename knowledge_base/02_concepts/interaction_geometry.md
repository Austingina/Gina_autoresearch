# Interaction Geometry

Interaction geometry refers to task-relevant geometric relationships among the end effector, object, support surface, target region, and scene constraints.

In this project, G-like signals may include:

- end-effector to object relative pose
- object to target relation
- contact/visibility/action affordance geometry
- mask/depth-derived spatial cues
- temporal change in interaction-relevant geometry

Working principle: G should be treated as a representation whose reliability matters, not as a guaranteed privileged feature.
